## r/ItalyExpats / r/expats (English)

**Title:** I built a free map of official rent prices by neighbourhood for 15 Italian cities

If you're moving to Italy (or within it), figuring out what rent costs in each neighbourhood is painful: listings are noisy and Numbeo only gives a city average.

The Italian Revenue Agency (Agenzia delle Entrate) publishes official rent ranges for every zone of every town twice a year, but they're buried in a clunky form. I put them on a map:

👉 https://giammy92.github.io/affittiitalia/

- type an address → see the zone's official €/m² monthly rent range
- set your flat size → get a monthly estimate
- set a budget → see which zones fit

Cities: Milan, Rome, Bologna, Genoa, Palermo, Bari, Catania, Venice, Padua, Trieste, Trento, Cagliari, Pescara, Rimini, Pisa. Site is in Italian, but it's mostly numbers.

Caveat: these are official reference values, and current asking rents in popular areas are often 15–30% higher. Free, no sign-up, no cookies. Feedback very welcome: does it match what you're seeing?

## What to watch in replies

- "The prices look low" → expected (OMI vs. asking rents). Strong signal that people care about *current market* data, which points to the crowdsourced layer (Approach C in the May design doc).
- "Add <city>" → a direct city request. Count them; it's the cheapest demand signal there is.
- Silence and fewer than 30% of visitors searching → the wedge isn't landing. Rethink before building more.
